# Stage gaps

Headings that show their placeholder (the heading's emoji, big on the paper) because the card has
nothing of its own for them yet: no suitable picture was found (licensed CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA, the
card's own species, wild, no people, nothing distressing), or the card has no data for it. Each row
says what could fill the gap, so that every card ends up with something of its own to show.
Add rows as each heading is done; remove a row when its gap is filled.

Not published: `docs/` isn't in the site's allow-list (`scripts/build-site.mjs`).
